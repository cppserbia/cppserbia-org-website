# C++ Serbia in Novi Sad

Hi everyone!

C++ Serbia is heading north! On **Tuesday, 24th of November**, we are bringing a C++ evening to **Novi Sad**, hosted at the **Startit AI Hub** in the city center.

The talks and speakers will be announced soon. Expect C++ talks, time for questions, and plenty of time afterwards to meet the C++ developers of Novi Sad.

## Who Should Attend

Whether you write C++ for a living, are just starting out, or are simply curious about what the language looks like today, you are welcome. If you have been meaning to come to one of our meetups but Belgrade was too far, this one is for you.
