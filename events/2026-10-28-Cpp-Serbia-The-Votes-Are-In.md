# C++ Serbia: The Votes Are In

Hi everyone!

This Wednesday we are inviting you to a **casual beer meetup** at **Dogma Brewery & Tap Room**: no talk, no slides, just C++ folks, cold beer and a week's worth of votes to talk about.

It has been a week of ballots. On Sunday, Serbia went to the polls in record numbers. A few days earlier, the ISO ballot on the C++26 draft closed and came back **Disapproved**. So this Wednesday we are doing what every committee does after a vote: meeting over a drink to argue about it. 🍺

## 🗳️ On the Table

- **C++26 and contracts** — The national bodies voted the C++26 draft down: 12 of 20 in favor where 14 were needed, and 9 of 22 voting No, well over the 25% allowed. Contracts look like the sticking point. Re-ballot as is? Pull contracts out? Ship it as C++27? WG21 meets in Búzios in November to decide, so come cast your (non-binding) vote first.
- **Latest breakthroughs in scheduling algorithms** — From kernel schedulers to work-stealing runtimes and `std::execution`. Read a paper you can't stop thinking about? Bring it.
- **Whatever else is current** — Your latest compiler bug, a war story from production, or a topic of your own. The floor is open.

## What to Expect

- **Casual conversation** — No slides, no agenda, no microphones. Just C++ folks hanging out over a beer.
- **Meet the community** — Whether you are a regular or this is your first time, come say hello.
- **Opinions welcome** — Strong ones especially. Unlike the ISO ballot, nothing decided at our table is binding.

## Who Should Attend

If you love C++, or you love beer, or you simply love hanging out with C++ developers (?!), you are welcome!
