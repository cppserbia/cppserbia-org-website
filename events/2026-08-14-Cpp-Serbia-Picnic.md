# C++ Serbia Picnic

Hi everyone!

**We're calling it a picnic. Bring an appetite anyway — the grill is on.** 🔥

Forget the lecture hall: this August we are trading slides for smoke. C++ Serbia is firing up a proper **roštilj** in **Košutnjak**, under the trees, and you are invited. Same spot as last time, same hour, considerably more charcoal.

Coals lit at 18:00. First ćevapi off the grill shortly after. We keep the fire going until the meat runs out or the sun does — whichever comes first.

## Two Kinds of Grilling

Let's be honest about what this event is.

**The first kind** happens over charcoal, and produces ćevapi.

**The second kind** happens the moment you mention, out loud and within earshot, that you have _"pretty much got a handle on move semantics."_ Someone will put down their plate. Someone will ask what happens to a moved-from `std::vector`. Someone else will bring up guaranteed copy elision, and then it is 22:00 and the coals are cold and you are drawing object lifetimes on a napkin.

Both are done at medium-high heat. Both are best enjoyed with a cold drink. We are providing the equipment for one of them and the audience for the other.

Nobody leaves undercooked.

## What's on the Grill

- **Ćevapi, pljeskavice, and sausages** — off the coals, straight onto your plate.
- **Grilled veggies and halloumi** — a full setup for everyone who doesn't do meat. You will not be stuck eating bread and salad.
- **Salads, bread, ajvar, kajmak** — the mandatory supporting cast.
- **Cold drinks** — beer and non-alcoholic, in the cooler, in the shade.

Tell us about any dietary requirements when you RSVP and we will make sure there is something on the grill for you.

## Who Should Attend

Anyone who writes C++, used to write C++, wants to write C++, or simply enjoys the company of people who do. First-timers especially welcome — it is a lot easier to say hello over a grill than across a lecture hall, and we promise the questions are friendlier than the ones in a technical interview.

Bring your hardest C++ question. Bring your worst production war story. Bring an opinion about how the meat should be turned — everyone else will have one too, and that argument is at least as old as the language.

## Headcount — Please RSVP

This one genuinely matters: **we buy the meat by the kilo based on your responses.** Respond here or on any of our social channels so nobody ends up staring at an empty grill. Ovaj put brojimo na kilograme. 🥩
