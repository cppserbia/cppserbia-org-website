# C++ Serbia && Microsoft Dev Center Serbia: Bit About IT Meetup

🇷🇸 C++ Serbia `&&` Microsoft Dev Center Serbia 🤝

This October, C++ Serbia heads across the river to New Belgrade! We are teaming up with our friends from Microsoft Dev Center Serbia for a joint evening in their _Bit About IT_ meetup series, hosted on the 11th floor of Ušće Tower 2.

We have three topics delivered by Ivan Čukić, Bojan Janjić and Maksim Vlasov. Each talk runs for about 20 minutes, with short breaks in between to stretch your legs, grab a refreshment and discuss what you have just heard. After the final talk, we invite you to stay for drinks and socializing, a great opportunity to meet the speakers, catch up with the community and get to know the people behind some of the C++ written right here in Belgrade.

## 🎤 Talks

- **"Looks wacky, but it works"** — Ivan Čukić
- **How we run C++ shared layout lib in Web** — Bojan Janjić
- **Transferrable interfaces with `box<InterfaceT>`** — Maksim Vlasov
